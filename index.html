About

Builder.
Shipper.
Player.

I'm Sonujeet, a Mumbai-based maker. Building apps and games with AI is my hobby, and I ship them to the web, Android and Windows.

I like the part where an idea becomes something you can tap, play and send to a friend the same day. I work on the whole thing: the game feel, the interface, the multiplayer, the build for every platform, and the page you download it from. I update in public on itch.io and fix what players actually hit.

Off-screen I'm learning jewellery CAD in Matrix 9.0. Different medium, same love for precise geometry.

How I build with AI

Idea → Build → Ship
  1. 01 · Idea

    A game I want to play

    Every project starts as something I want on my own phone: a boss fight, a chess app, a scary night at the station.

  2. 02 · Build with AI

    Describe, build, steer

    I describe what I want, build it with AI, and keep steering until the feel is right.

  3. 03 · Ship

    Play-test and ship

    I play it again and again, fix what bugs me, then ship to the web, Android and Windows.
