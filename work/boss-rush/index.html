BOSS RUSH

Six heroes. Seven bosses. One more run.

Role
Design, code, animation, audio integration, builds
Platforms
Web · Android
Built with
Three.js · WebGL · JavaScript · Android WebView · Realtime co-op server
Current version
v1.7 · 2026

The idea

A 3D roguelite boss rush that runs in the browser and on Android. Pick a hero, read the wind-up, dodge on the last frame, draft an upgrade, and walk into the next arena. Bring friends for an online co-op raid.

Play on itch.io →Play in browser →

What's inside

01

6 heroes

Blade, Guardian, Berserker, Storm Monk, Ranger and Arcanist, each with their own combo, special and ultimate.

02

7 bosses

Warden, Witch, Stalker, Echo, Dragon, Golem and the secret Lamplighter, with phases and readable wind-ups.

03

Modes

Boss Rush, Story, Tower with boons, a Weekly Raid, practice and daily challenges.

04

Online co-op

Raid a boss together over a realtime server, with leaderboards.

05

Fight feel

Procedural animation layer: anticipation, step-in strikes, hit-stop, staggers, camera punch and haptics.

06

Built for phones

Landscape-first touch controls, High and Low quality, English and Hindi.
