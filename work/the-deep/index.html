THE DEEP

A research station went silent. You dive alone.

Role
Design, code, 3D models, audio, builds
Platforms
Web · Android · Windows
Built with

Current version

The idea

A 3D underwater survival mystery made in Godot 4 with Blender models. The research station VARUNA-9 has gone silent, and you dive alone to find out why. Swim from a sunlit reef through a wreck field into the Laxmi Trench, manage your oxygen and battery, solve the station's puzzles and piece the story together from 18 voiced logs.

What's inside

01

Three zones

A sunlit reef, a wreck field and the Laxmi Trench, from god rays down to total darkness.

02

A 5-part station

Explore the flooded modules of VARUNA-9, restore its power and find out why it went silent.

03

Things in the water

Stalker eels, drifting jellyfish and a 28 m Leviathan waiting in the trench.

04

Puzzles & 18 voiced logs

Route power, crack codes and turn valves, and hear the crew's story in their own voices.

05

Survive the dive

Oxygen and battery to manage, scrap to collect, and an upgrade bench for your gear.

06

3 endings

Your discoveries decide how it ends. v1.0.1 fixed 25 bugs and added multi-touch phone controls.
